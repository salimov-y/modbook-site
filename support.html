Frequently asked

How do I sync my garage across devices?

Sign in to iCloud on each device and make sure iCloud is enabled for Modbook. Your cars, mods, service records, and photos sync automatically through your private iCloud account — no separate login needed.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud. Modbook has no analytics or trackers, and the developer cannot see your data. See our Privacy Policy.

Does Modbook work for electric cars?

Yes. Set your car's powertrain to electric and Modbook adapts — battery and charging service tracking, and no oil-change reminders. Petrol, diesel, hybrid, and plug-in hybrid are all supported too.

What are dyno and Dragy runs?

Modbook logs both dyno pulls (power, torque, boost) and Dragy-style acceleration times (0–100, 100–200, quarter mile). Each run remembers which mods were installed when it was measured.

I deleted something by accident. Can I get it back?

Modbook never hard-deletes — removed items are kept as hidden records rather than erased. If you need to recover something, email support and we'll help.
